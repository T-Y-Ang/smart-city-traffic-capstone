# Annual Traffic Volume Observations

## Observation 1: Large increase from 2012 to 2013

Total recorded traffic volume increased from 8,208,767 in 2012 to 28,177,412 in 2013, representing an increase of 19,968,645 (243.26%).

However, the data coverage analysis shows that 2012 contains only 2,559 records and begins on 2 October 2012, whereas 2013 contains 8,573 records covering the full calendar year. 

Therefore, the large increase should not be interpreted solely as an actual increase in traffic. The difference in data coverage is likely to contribute substantially to the higher total recorded in 2013.

## Observation 2: Decline in 2014 and 2015 followed by a rebound in 2016

Total recorded traffic volume decreased by 44.17% from 2013 to 2014 and by a further 9.85% from 2014 to 2015. It then increased by 107.99% from 2015 to 2016. This brings it back to almost the levels seen prior to the decline.

The coverage analysis again shows that these totals are affected by differences in the number of observations. The 2014 data ends in August, while the 2015 data begins in June. In contrast, 2016 contains records from January through December. 

Therefore, the apparent decline and subsequent increase cannot be attributed entirely to changes in actual traffic conditions.

## Additional observation

Between 2016 and 2017, total recorded traffic volume increased from 29,494,821 to 35,428,156, an increase of 5,933,335 (20.12%). 

Both years contain records spanning January through December, making this comparison more suitable than comparisons involving clearly partial years. 

However, the number of observations still differs between the two years, so the increase should still be interpreted cautiously.
