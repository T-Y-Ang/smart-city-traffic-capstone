# Holiday Temperature and Traffic Observations

## Labor Day

Average temperature on Labor Day was 295.32 K in 2015, 295.01 K in 2016, and 291.29 K in 2017.

From 2015 to 2016, the average temperature decreased slightly by 0.32 K. During the same period, average traffic volume decreased by 278.75 from 2,430.29 to 2,151.54.

From 2016 to 2017, the average temperature decreased more noticeably by 3.72 K. However, average traffic volume increased by 243.63 from 2,151.54 to 2,395.17.

Therefore, the Labor Day results do not show a consistent relationship between year-on-year temperature changes and traffic volume. In particular, traffic increased from 2016 to 2017 despite the lower average temperature.

## New Years Day

A comparison between 2015 and the later years cannot be made because the dataset does not contain records for January 2015.

For the available years, average temperature increased by 4.76 K from 267.09 K in 2016 to 271.85 K in 2017. Average traffic volume also increased by 259.08 from 1,832.00 to 2,091.08.

Although both temperature and traffic increased between these two observations, this is insufficient to establish that the higher temperature caused the increase in traffic.

## Data limitations and interpretation

The holiday analysis contains only a small number of holiday dates. Furthermore, New Years Day 2016 contains 18 unique hourly observations rather than a complete set of 24, and January 2015 is absent from the dataset.

The results should therefore be interpreted as descriptive observations rather than evidence of a causal relationship between temperature and traffic volume. The available Labor Day results also demonstrate that traffic volume does not consistently move in the same direction as temperature.
